# Anvil

Workout timer and training log — a single-file, offline-first PWA.

**→ https://jpasserin.github.io/anvil/**

Open it on your phone and *Add to Home Screen*. It installs as a standalone app,
works offline, and keeps everything in `localStorage` on your device — no
account, no server, nothing leaves the phone.

- Build a library of exercises, each with its own duration and illustration
- Tap one to start its timer; the minutes are logged when it finishes
- Chain exercises into a routine and it walks you through them with rests
- Browse the history day by day, week by week, month by month, year by year

This repo is the **published build only** — five files, served by GitHub Pages.
Source, tests and dev tooling live in a separate private repo.
