Goals open the Goals tab ›

Weekly targets live on the Goals tab, one per goal. Changing one applies from this week on and leaves earlier weeks alone, so a medal you've earned stays earned. The month and year targets only count from your first day, so a year you joined in July isn't judged against all twelve months.

You

Age, height and sex are only used locally to work out BMI and the Mifflin-St Jeor resting-burn formula. Leaving sex unset takes the midpoint of the two constants, which costs the estimate about 80 kcal/day. Desk and sleep hours shape the daily-burn estimate on the Body page.
